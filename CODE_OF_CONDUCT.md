# Code of conduct

Lower Duck Pond Hosting should be a welcoming, constructive place to collaborate.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and mistakes with patience and respect;
- critique work without attacking people;
- respect fictional and real-world identity boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and unwanted disclosure of private
  information;
- keep role-play from obscuring security, consent, or operational decisions.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior undermines a safe and productive community. Report conduct concerns
privately through the channel in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) when public
discussion would expose sensitive information.
